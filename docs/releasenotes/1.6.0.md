# v1.6.0 — macOS support, 20 new app icons, and smoother profile editing

AWS Profile Manager now runs on **macOS** as well as Windows. You can also pick a new app icon from
twenty designs, see credential problems at a glance on the tray icon, and edit profiles with less
scrolling.

Your current icon stays exactly as it is unless you choose a new one.

---

## 🍎 macOS

- Runs natively on **Apple silicon and Intel** Macs, downloadable as a `.dmg`.
- Native window controls, a menu-bar icon, and the Dock icon reopens the window.
- The embedded terminal opens your login shell (zsh by default), so `aws` is on your `PATH` just
  as it is in Terminal.
- One-click console sign-in can use any browser installed in Applications.
- Updates install automatically, the same as on Windows.

## 🎨 Choose your app icon

- Pick from **20 new designs** under *Settings → Appearance → App icon*, in styles from Bauhaus and
  8-bit to synthwave, claymorphism and frosted glass.
- The change applies immediately to the window, the taskbar, the Dock, the tray and the splash
  screen, with no restart.
- **Classic** (the current icon) stays the default and is always available.
- The installer, the Start menu and pinned shortcuts keep the Classic icon.

## 🚦 Credential status on the tray icon

- A small dot on the tray icon shows when something needs attention:
  - **Amber**: credentials for an auto-refresh profile are about to expire.
  - **Red**: auto refresh stopped after repeated failures.
  - **Gray**: auto refresh is paused.
- Hover over the tray icon to see the reason.
- Turn the dot off under *Settings → Appearance* if you don't want it.
- New **monochrome** tray style: just the symbol, in the same color as the system icons next to it.
  It switches between light and dark with your taskbar.

## ✏️ Easier profile editing

- **Save** and **Cancel** now appear at the top of the edit form as well as the bottom, so you no
  longer have to scroll down to finish.
- Leaving the Profiles screen while editing now cancels the edit. When you come back you see your
  profile list, and nothing was saved.

---

## Upgrading

No action needed. Profiles, settings and saved credentials carry over, and your app icon doesn't
change unless you pick a new one.
