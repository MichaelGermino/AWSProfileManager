# v1.5.0 — Identity Center, one-click console, and profile folders

Adds AWS accounts reached through **IAM Identity Center** (federated to Entra ID) alongside your
existing SAML/ADFS profiles, one-click console sign-in, folders for organizing profiles, and an
optional video background.

Existing profiles are unaffected and need no migration.

---

## ✨ IAM Identity Center

- Sign in through your browser **once per organization** — eight profiles means one prompt, not
  eight. No admin setup needed.
- **Bulk import**: sign in, tick the accounts and permission sets you want, get profiles.
- Credentials land in `~/.aws/credentials` just like SAML profiles do.
- Auto-refresh never interrupts you. When a session genuinely needs you, it asks and waits.

## 🔐 One-click AWS console

- A button on every profile, plus an **AWS Console** tray submenu — both account types.
- Refreshes expired credentials for you first.
- **Several accounts signed in at once** — no more "You must first log out".
- Opens in the browser you choose, or an in-app window kept separate per profile.

## 📁 Profile folders

- Group profiles into folders you name and color.
- Drag profiles in and out, or **right-click a profile** and pick a folder — quicker than dragging
  when the list is long.
- Collapse folders you aren't using, and drag folders themselves to reorder.
- Deleting a folder never deletes profiles — they move back to Ungrouped.

## 🎨 Custom background

- Play a looping video behind the app instead of the plain grey — *Settings → Appearance*.
- Bring your own MP4 or WebM, or use the one included.
- Apply it to the sign-in screen only, or to the whole app with translucent panels over it.
- A slider sets how much blur sits behind those panels, from clear through to frosted.
- A button on the sign-in screen turns it off and back on without opening Settings.

## 🧭 Setup wizard

- Asks how you sign in, then shows only the steps that apply.
- **Bulk SAML import**, matching the Identity Center one.
- **Organization configuration files** — an admin exports a `.json` from *Settings → General* and
  shares it; new users load it and everything is prefilled. No secrets inside, and it can be
  imported at any time.

## 🔒 Security

- **The master password now covers Identity Center too** — it encrypts stored sessions and locks
  the app on restart.
- While locked, the tray is disabled and nothing refreshes in the background.
- **"Forget master password" signs you out of your identity provider**, so getting AWS credentials
  again means signing in properly.

## ⚡ Faster startup

Cold start is down from about 15 seconds to under 2.

---

## 🐛 Fixes

- Renaming a profile's credentials section now moves the credentials with it instead of stranding
  them.
- Account display names apply immediately, and settings changed elsewhere in the app stay changed.
- Settings is tabbed instead of one long scroll.

## Upgrading

No action needed — profiles, settings and saved credentials carry over. New defaults (console opens
in your browser, launch at startup on) apply to fresh installs only.
