# v2.0.0-beta.4 — native rewrite (beta)

**This is a test build of AWS Profile Manager 2.0.** You are seeing it because *Allow pre-release*
is turned on in Settings. If you would rather stay on the stable version, reinstall 1.6.1 from the
releases page: your profiles, folders, settings and saved sign-ins are kept either way.

---

## ↔️ Resizable Terminal panes

- Drag the edges between the command explorer, the terminal, the AI assistant and the command
  details to resize them, as in 1.x.
- The side panes keep their proportions when you resize the window, and the terminal always keeps
  enough room.
- Your layout, including collapsed panes, is remembered the next time you open the app.

## ▶️ Resume auto refresh from the Profiles page

- When auto refresh is paused, the banner on the Profiles page now has a **Resume auto refresh**
  button. No more trip to Settings.

## 🖼️ App icon in the taskbar

- Picking a new app icon now updates the taskbar button right away, not only after a restart.
